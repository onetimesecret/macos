the sheet, live — the window round

The panel grew up into a window: drag it by the title bar, resize it by any edge, double-click the title to stretch it tall. Page tabs moved to the bottom edge, Excel-style — ⌘1–9 jumps, ⌥⌘←/→ steps, drag to reorder, double-click a tab to pause its clock (1h, then 24h, then top-up). ⌘0 opens the ledger — a permanent dashed tab where expired and closed pages rest as dimmed ink. Sealed paste rekeyed to ⇧⌘V (⌥⌘V is taken by the OS); sealing anything already on the page is now ⌘↩ — the selection, or the current line. And the page reads markdown: ### heading renders like one without ever hiding the markup. The rules are written down in the interaction-model spec, revision C.
