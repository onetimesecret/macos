v9 · the sheet — uni-cell as a little text file

v8 priced the uni-cell as a single-occupant chamber. v9 lets the chamber be a page: paste, come back later, hit enter twice, paste again — the actual life of the random text file this app replaces. Still one cell, one lifetime; what changes is its grain. A sheet holds three kinds of ink: typed text (yours, readable — you wrote it), benign pastes (readable — recognition was never the problem), and sealed chips — anything secret-shaped lands masked, labelled, atomic, because the bytes still never reach the UI. So auto-labelling survives per snippet, on the chips; the page itself needs no caption. Create-link exists at both grains: a quiet ↗ on every chip, one for the whole page in the footer. Focus is the other spec collision: a page you type into must sometimes hold the keyboard — the rule refines to accept, never take (1c). Sheets can multiply Excel-style — several exist, one shows: 1d tabs, 1e pad edges, 1f bare flip — three switchers so we don't marry the tab strip.

constants (spec, unchanged): sealed chips are masked, never revealable · hover reveals actions, never content · one countdown per sheet — snippets never get timers · ladder 1h→3h→8h→24h→3d→7d, default 8h · excluded from capture · no notifications — a hidden sheet dies quietly

The flagged features, re-priced on a page

masking · boundary law
intact for pastes: secret-shaped content lands already sealed — bytes are routed core-side before render, no flash of plaintext (1b) · the new edge: a secret typed by hand is visible by definition — a quiet per-run "seal this?" offer is the open question
create link
two grains — ↗ on every chip for one snippet · ↗ page in the footer bundles the whole sheet into one secret, expiry seeded from the page's remaining time
ttl ladder · click-to-cycle
per sheet — one label, one countdown, everything on the page expires together; per-snippet timers are exactly the futility trap uni-cell exists to kill
hover → actions, never content
survives — chips are the only hover targets; typed text has no actions to hide
never steals focus
refines to accept, never take — the keyboard enters only by deliberate click or ⌥Space, esc always hands it back · chips stay atomic under the caret: one ⌫ removes a whole chip, selection cannot reach inside one
capacity 12 · refuse-don't-evict
re-bases to sheets — a few pages, one visible; the cap question moves up a level (wall at 3? 5?) and snippets-per-page needs no cap at all
the v8 backbreakers, re-checked
clutter: one page in view · ttl futility: one countdown per view — other sheets' time reads as tab/edge geometry, never as numbers to track · fiddliness: chips are atomic and finger-sized, the page body is one big target · and the second pocket returns, without multi-cell chrome: ad-hoc separation on one page
