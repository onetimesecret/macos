v8 · uni-cell — one chamber, the text-file ritual

v7's six directions all answered one question — how many countdowns stay legible at once. Your notes call the question itself the defect: six cells is a wall of information however the time drains; six concurrent TTLs make tracking feel futile, so caring decays; and every cell seeds five-to-ten fine targets until the cursor is a bull in a china shop. Uni-cell sets capacity to 1 and ports the ritual this app actually replaces — the random empty text file kept open all day: paste over it, copy out of it, close without saving. The ledger below re-prices every committed feature at capacity 1; the frames then take the decisions that genuinely remain: the panel's form (1a, 1b), the one hard policy — paste while occupied (1a replace · 1c exchange · 1d wall), retrieval (1f), and how small the furniture can get (1e, 1g). Multi-cell stays alive in v7 for comparison.

constants (spec, unchanged): masked recognition, no plaintext in the ui · hover reveals actions, never content · no bin, no history, no notifications · excluded from screen capture · ladder 1h→3h→8h→24h→3d→7d, default 8h · ember #D45A2A
