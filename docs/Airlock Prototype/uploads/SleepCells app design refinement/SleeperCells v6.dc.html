6New page · time as geometry, resizable window, Recently deleted

The passage of time now uses the one thing every cell shares: its width. The full width is the horizon N (24 h by default) — a cell with 12 h left fills half-way; anything beyond N pegs at full and stops mattering visually. A thin column on the right edge is the slow axis (N×7, a week) draining downward. The label on each cell is the TTL rung in use (age since added lives in its tooltip); hover a cell to see the exact remaining time as a watermark countdown — click it to keep it visible. The window resizes by its right edge, bottom edge, and bottom corners. “Expired” is renamed Recently deleted and works like the Trash: × sends a cell there, everything there leaves after 24 h, deleting takes two clicks, and the list is always reachable — even when empty.

DESIGN NOTES
  • Resize. Right edge, bottom edge, and both bottom corners drag to resize. (Top/left stay anchored — the real panel docks to the screen edge.)
  • Time as geometry. The full cell width is the horizon N (default 24 h). Half the width = N/2 left. Beyond N the line pegs full — by design, 2×N vs 7×N doesn’t matter visually. The thin right-edge column is the slow axis (up to N×7): it shows only the time beyond N, so at N or less it is empty and the width line alone carries the story. Ember only when under an hour.
  • The label is the rung. The small label shows the TTL in use (8h, 3d…) and stays put; clicking cycles it, and the new rung pops up above the label so the cursor never hides it. Age since added lives in the tooltip; the exact remaining time appears on hover as a watermark countdown — click the watermark to keep it visible after the cursor leaves, click again to release — d hh:mm:ss, like a stopwatch. Tap-and-hold would do the same on touch (not built here).
  • Recently deleted, not “Expired”. It behaves like the Trash: × sends a cell there, expiry sends it there, everything leaves after 24 h unless restored. The time shown inside is since the cell expired or was removed.
  • Always reachable. The “0 deleted” button stays visible even when empty — some people need to see the empty list to trust it’s empty.
  • Two-click delete. Below Restore: first click arms (“Really delete?”), second click deletes. It disarms itself after a few seconds.
  • Tweaks (panel on the right): slow-axis on the left instead of right · different N for the width · different multiples of N for the column · or swap both bars for a small 2-D plot per cell (across = hours axis, up = week axis; the dot walks toward the corner as time runs out).
  • Carried from v5: no auto-sort (button/⌘S cycles time left / A–Z, dragging returns to manual order) · empty cells valid · on-deck staging · editing or copying restarts the clock · sync cloud · pin.
