# Product design

## Operational notebook

The [operational-notebook design](operational-notebook/README.md) is the full
product design-change track authorized on 2026-11-14. Its dossier contains the
working thesis, conceptual model and diagrams, scenarios, and transition gates.
Detailed behaviors remain proposed until individually accepted; preparation is
not a shipped redesign.

The [implementation preparation plan](../../plans/operational-notebook.md) and
[epic #358](https://github.com/onetimesecret/macos/issues/358) track the staged work.

## Timeline tabs

The [timeline-tabs design](timeline-tabs/README.md) remains available as the
preceding design corpus. Its applicable accepted decisions are not implicitly
superseded by the new direction. Specific changes require explicit decisions;
the [transition register](operational-notebook/transition.md) identifies the
first questions.
